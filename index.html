01 / Why

Built on files you own, running on a server you control.

Phone

Claude Code in your pocket

Chat with your own Claude over Telegram, wherever you are. It reaches your vault, mail and WhatsApp, and asks before acting with an Allow / Deny button.

Ownership

Not locked to any AI

Everything you know lives in plain Markdown files that you own. If a better model comes along, point it at the same folder. Nothing needs exporting.

Speed

Fast, because it is all local

Your notes are plain files on your laptop, so your agent searches thousands of them in milliseconds. No database or app in the way, which makes it much faster and more capable.

Tasks

Task management built in

Tasks, priorities and schedules live in your vault. Talk through what is on your mind, and Claude turns it into tasks, helps you prioritise and fits them into your week.

Capture

One button to capture

The iPhone Action Button or a Mac hotkey drops an idea, note or task into your Inbox in two seconds. Send it to your Telegram bot instead, and Claude starts on it straight away.

Server

Works overnight, laptop off

The server keeps going when you sleep: backups, health checks and tidy-ups out of the box. Add your own jobs with Claude, such as sorting mail, a morning briefing or background work on tasks.

03 / Your week

Not a notes app. An assistant that knows your goals, time and commitments.

3.1

It messages you. You tap once.

Claude can message you on Telegram when something needs you. Have it watch for what you care about, like sign-ups for a speaker you would love to hear. It messages you, you reply "sign me up", and before it acts it asks for your OK with one tap.

3.2

Your ideal week, planned for you.

Describe it once: wake and bed times, focus blocks, sport, classes, friends. Claude schedules your tasks, assignments and readings around it, reads your calendars, says plainly when the week is overbooked, and writes the blocks into your calendar once you approve.

3.3

Priorities that mean something.

A North Star note holds what matters this quarter. Rate Health, Work, Play and Love, set an objective for each, then rank three to five priorities. Every prioritisation pass reads it first, so urgent never quietly beats important.

04 / Memory

The longer you use it, the better it knows you.

Wiki

A second brain that maintains itself

After Karpathy's LLM wiki: Claude compiles your notes, readings and lessons into a small wiki, and answers from what you yourself concluded, with citations.

Profile

Memory you can read and edit

Dated notes of your preferences, routines, people and goals. Claude adds to them as you work. Works with any model.

Code

Fully yours to change

Plain files and readable code. Drop what you do not need; build what fits you.

07 / Privacy

Every account is yours.

Anthropic

What Claude reads, Anthropic processes

Your notes, and mail and WhatsApp if you turn them on. Avoiding this would need a local model, which the kit does not support.

Server

Everything else is locked down

Reachable only through your private Tailscale network. Keys in locked files. Backups encrypted before they leave.

András

Sees nothing

No access to your server, vault or accounts.

WhatsApp

Optional, off by default

It uses an unofficial connection, which can get a number banned.
